# 👋🏾 Welcome!

I'm **Shaurita D. Hutchins**, a computational biologist and PhD candidate in Genetics, Genomics, and Bioinformatics at UAB.

**I build systems that help people understand biology.**

My work spans computational biology, research software, knowledge systems, education, and advocacy. I design tools, workflows, and frameworks that help scientists and communities organize, interpret, apply, and reuse complex biological information.

I build these systems with diversity, equity, inclusion, and accessibility in mind, seeking to expand who can participate in, understand, and benefit from science.

My research currently focuses on genetic modifiers and regulatory networks in rare diseases, particularly heritable pulmonary arterial hypertension.
